Add `generateRole(color)` to `@obosbbl/grunnmuren-tailwind/theme`. It makes every variant of a role from one colour, with the lightness of Grunnmuren's own roles so the contrast pairs hold. `CONTRAST_PAIRS` and `contrastRatio` are exported too, for checking every pair and not just the ones a theme changes.
